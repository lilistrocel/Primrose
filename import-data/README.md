## 📊 Food Processing Standards

We distinguish between healthy **minimal processing** and harmful **ultra-processing**:

### ✅ **Fresh & Whole Foods** (Categories 1-11)
- **Zero processing**: Foods in their natural state
- **Examples**: Fresh vegetables, whole grains, raw nuts, fresh fish
- **Benefits**: Maximum nutrients, no additives, natural fiber intact

### ✅ **Minimally Processed Foods** (Categories 12-14)
- **Traditional processing**: Methods used for centuries for preservation and digestibility
- **Simple ingredients**: Recognizable, whole food ingredients only
- **Examples**: Plain yogurt, steel-cut oats, frozen vegetables, canned tomatoes
- **Standards**: No artificial additives, preservatives, or flavor enhancers
- **Benefits**: Improved digestibility, extended shelf life, retained nutritional value

### 🚫 **Ultra-Processed Foods** (NOT INCLUDED)
- **Industrial processing**: Factory-made with artificial ingredients
- **Examples**: Protein bars, flavored yogurts with additives, instant noodles, processed meats
- **Characteristics**: Multiple artificial ingredients, high sodium/sugar, preservatives
- **Why excluded**: Linked to health issues, poor nutritional quality

All CSV files follow these standards:
- **Fresh foods**: Farm-direct with zero processing, maximum nutritional value
- **Minimally processed foods**: Traditional methods only, simple ingredients, no artificial additives
- **USDA-accurate nutritional data** per 100g for whole and minimally processed foods
- **Evidence-based health benefits** and risks for natural and traditional foods
- **Realistic storage requirements** and shelf life for fresh and preserved products
- **Proper seasonality** and geographic origins from actual farming regions
- **Schema-compliant format** matching `schema.md`

## 🚫 What We DON'T Include

- **Ultra-processed foods**: Protein bars, flavored snacks, instant meals
- **Artificial additives**: Foods with preservatives, artificial colors, flavors
- **High-sugar products**: Sweetened cereals, flavored yogurts, candy
- **Heavily processed meats**: Hot dogs, lunch meats, bacon with nitrates
- **Refined/enriched products**: White bread, sugary cereals, refined oils

## 🌱 What We DO Include

#### **Fresh Foods:**
- **Fresh vegetables & fruits**: Straight from farms and orchards
- **Whole grains**: Unprocessed kernels, berries, and seeds
- **Dried legumes**: Naturally dried beans, lentils, and peas
- **Fresh proteins**: Farm-raised meat, fresh-caught fish, farm eggs
- **Raw nuts & seeds**: Unroasted, unsalted varieties

#### **Minimally Processed Foods:**
- **Traditional fermented foods**: Plain yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi
- **Simple preserved foods**: Canned tomatoes, frozen vegetables, dried fruits
- **Traditional preparations**: Steel-cut oats, nut butters, cold-pressed oils
- **Artisanal products**: Aged cheeses, traditional cured meats, fermented vegetables

## 📋 CSV Format

Each CSV contains these key fields:
- Basic info: name, description, category, subcategory
- Nutrition: calories, macros, vitamins, minerals (per 100g)
- Health: benefits, risks, dietary restrictions, allergens
- Storage: shelf life, temperature, conditions
- Meta: seasonality, origin, alternative names
